A metaphor is an image that carries a schema or concept that is hard to grasp at first. In terms of codings, a metaphor conveys meaning through an image. [Codings](?p=4.1.1)

- **Discourse as an island in the ocean.** On each island, a philosopher and their students develop a language of their own. Ships travel between the islands, carrying ideas from one discourse to another. [Discourse, excursus, cosmos](?p=2.7)
- **Life as a flow, situations as whirlpools.** A whirlpool forms where the usual flow of life breaks. A whirlpool that deserves attention is a situation. [Problems, tasks, questions](?p=4.8)
- **The transition from Euclid to Hilbert as extending rigid structures from the present into the future.** Making the rules explicit lets us carry them beyond familiar examples and agree on how to understand new cases. [Euclid and Hilbert](?p=2.6)
- **Communication as sending messages through different channels.** People can convey the same message in words, in a drawing, or with a gesture. [Communication and conflict](?p=5.8.1)
- **Self-directed learning as finding and unpacking memes in culture.** Learners seek out messages left by others and reconstruct their meaning. A **meme** in this image is a frozen message left by an unknown sender for anyone who wants to read it.
- **Deliberately changing levels as a pianist playing across the keyboard.** A pianist chooses keys as they play. In the same way, a person can choose a level of action to suit the task. [Levels of the field of attention](?p=3.7)
