Before you is Spiral Dynamics — a brief description of the levels: on the right, what a person feels; on the left, for which life conditions that is fitting.

Why this alternation of colors?
Odd ones are warm, even ones are cool.
Red and blue are the anchors.

Phenomenologically (especially on the first five levels) there is a fairly precise match with the TUAI levels.

True, there is no justification, and the vocabulary drifts — only a sense of it remains.

About the TUAI levels it is clear that the odd ones are individualists, the even ones are collectivists. Alexander Auzan, dean of the economics faculty at Moscow State University, calls this phenomenon in the Russian economy I-Russia and C-Russia.

The odd ones are stimulating, energetic, dynamic, but at times destructive.
The even ones are calming, stabilizing, harmonizing, but at times stagnant and decaying.

Ideal movement goes in two beats — first the odd one destroys the old and begins to build the new; the next even one finishes what was started and restores the broken ties with the outer world.

Such movement resolves the contradiction between individualism and collectivism in the best possible way.
