Between fathers and growing children a natural rivalry arises. They are in the same field and must prove to each other their professional fitness and status.

Between grandfather and grandson there is no such direct rivalry anymore.

The grandfather is no longer the commander. He takes the position of a **side expert**, who is allowed to notice the mistakes of the acting boss — the son — and discuss them with the grandson.

An interesting training setup emerges: the **son** on one side and **grandfather + grandson** on the other can play nearly as equals.

For the grandson this is a chance to practise dealing with the adult world, but without having to enter a real status fight with the grandfather.

The gap between generations creates what is hard to get in the pair “father — child”: a **serious game without the need for real competition**.
