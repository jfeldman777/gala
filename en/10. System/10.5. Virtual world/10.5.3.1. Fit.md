Suppose each person has their favorite levels (four out of eight).
Suppose you want to get a job. Every workplace has levels that
must be demonstrated (required ones).
Comparing favorite levels with desired ones, you can get five variants,
depending on how many of your favorite levels turned out to be required
(0 or 1 or 2 or 3 or 4)

(0) They will not even consider you for the post
(1) They will take you, but will try to fire you (as soon as there is a chance)
(2) They will take you and put up with you until you leave yourself — when you leave, they will breathe
a sigh of relief
(3) You like it here, they like you here, but you still have other favorite pursuits elsewhere
(4) You give yourself entirely to the work; you have no other interests

Two examples from my own experience
I = profile 3678
position “school teacher” = 2345
my wife = 2468

Guess which of us was thrown out after two years (twice, in fact), and which they put up with for eight years
