The Buddha left the palace and saw desire, suffering, pain.
Come with me — he said to a passer-by — we will teach you how to live and desire rightly so that it does not hurt.

I left home and saw that people choose badly — they simply do not see the whole picture, the full list of possibilities.
Come with me — I said — I will teach you to see the whole picture, and your choice will succeed. I will give you optics, and you will start seeing the details.

Much in common, isn't it? But the Buddha works through sensitivity, and I through sharpness of sight.

**A pedagogical system**

It seems I have reflection, logic, theory where the Buddha has pure empathy.

![[Pasted image 20260822153406.png]]

But look at the picture, at the key words.
The search for truth — that is 5.
Understanding — level 6.
Enlightenment — level 7.
Compassion — level 8.
What does this mean?
It means beginners are not shown the whole picture.
Beginners flounder between levels 1 and 3, and the Buddha treats them with the words WE and RIGHT — that is, levels 2 and 4, just as our theory of levels recommends.
But the Buddha does not impose free choice on them (as we often do), nor the higher levels at all — until they ask for that themselves.
